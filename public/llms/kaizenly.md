# A little better, every day.

Kaizenly is a **strict, local-first focus guard** for Android: pick a time, pick the apps that distract you, and start a session. Everything in the bag stays shut until the timer ends, and quitting early costs parachutes, so ending is a deliberate choice.

No download yet — the app is in development.

## Why "Kaizenly"?

The name comes from **Kaizen** — a Japanese idea meaning "change for the better." **Kai** is change and progress; **Zen** is calm, focus, and balance; and **ly** makes it a daily lifestyle.

## What it does — strict where it matters

- **Focus sessions** — bags, presets, the wheel, and a block screen that does not let go — with breaks and parachutes for when life interrupts.
- **Routines** — three routines that start themselves — on schedule, on the days you choose, each with its own bag.
- **The Tower** — five minutes a day lays a block. Seven blocks make a floor. Your streak, built where you can watch it grow.
- **Parachutes, earned free** — focus for 30 minutes a day and the streak pays you in parachutes — the first 10 days pay double. Never sold.

Everything is free. A custom card, 720-minute sessions, and silence / Do Not Disturb during sessions are all free, and there is no purchase in the app.

## Built by Nadeem

Kaizenly is built by **Nadeem Mazhar**, a solo developer, as **Nadeem Dev Apps**. It started as his own problem — a phone that would not stay down — and it is built the way he builds everything: local-first, strict where it matters, and honest about what it does.
