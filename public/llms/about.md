## The plan is the skill

Anyone can get AI to write code. The difference is the process: a PRD, a design doc, clear goals — then phases, built one by one, each one tested before the next starts.

PRD → Design doc → Goals → Phases → Tested, one by one.

I don't claim deep mastery of any one language — AI writes the code; I own the plan, the design, and the testing.

## Questions, answered

### Who is Nadeem Mazhar?

Nadeem Mazhar is a solo developer who runs Nadeem Dev Apps, a one-developer studio with no partners and no team. He plans, designs, and ships websites, Windows apps, Android apps, and backends, and his apps Tsumly and Kaizenly started as his own problems.

### What does Nadeem build?

Nadeem builds websites, Windows apps, Android apps, and backends — local-first or cloud, whichever the project needs. His shipped apps are local-first by design, with no account, no server, and no ads, because that is the right call for a personal tool, not a limitation on what he can build.

### How does Nadeem work with AI?

Nadeem plans the product, designs the interface, directs the build, and reviews everything before it ships, with AI tools doing the heavy lifting under his direction. The decisions are his: he explores a problem until he understands it, documents a clear plan, and ships only when the result works in daily use.

### Are Nadeem's apps free to use?

Yes. Everything Nadeem makes is free to use right now — including Kaizenly's custom sessions of 3 to 12 hours. There is nothing to pay for.
