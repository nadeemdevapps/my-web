# An animated cafe site with a menu and cart.

Browse the menu, add dishes to a cart, and send the order to the cafe as a WhatsApp message. The site is fully animated, and the whole flow works end to end.

Live site: https://brew-and-bean.nadeem-devapps.workers.dev/

## The ordering flow, piece by piece

- **A menu with prices in PKR** — the cafe menu is listed with prices in PKR, so what you see is what the order will cost.
- **A cart that holds your order** — add dishes as you browse, and review the full order — dishes and total — before you send it.
- **Orders by WhatsApp** — there is no checkout form and no account. Your order is sent straight to the cafe as a WhatsApp message.
- **Fully animated** — the site is animated throughout — the menu, the cart, and the ordering steps move as you use them.
- **A site, not an app** — it runs in the browser. There is nothing to install — open the live site and the menu is there.
- **Works end to end** — the flow is complete: browse, add to cart, and send the order.

## How it works

1. **Browse the menu.** Look through the dishes with their prices in PKR.
2. **Add to the cart.** Add what you want and check the order before sending.
3. **Send it on WhatsApp.** The order goes to the cafe as a WhatsApp message — that is the whole checkout.
