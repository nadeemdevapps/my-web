## Selected work

### Tsumly

A **local-first money ledger** — every amount adds up. Track expenses and loans on your own device, with an optional **encrypted Google Drive backup**.

- **Smart multi-loan repayment split** — one repayment spread across your loans, the fair way.
- **Pre-app loans** — record loans from before the app without moving your balance.
- **Person profiles** — every loan tied to a person, with their totals at a glance.

[Open Tsumly's site](https://nadeem-dev-apps.pages.dev/products/tsumly/)

### Kaizenly

A **strict, local-first focus guard** — timed sessions that keep distracting apps shut when it matters.

- **Sessions ("bags")** — 15 min, 45 min, 3 h, or a custom length (3–12 h).
- **Parachute exit cost** — quitting early costs something, so it stays a deliberate choice.
- **Streaks, routines, and stats** — the progress building up, visible day by day.

[Open Kaizenly's site](https://nadeem-dev-apps.pages.dev/products/kaizenly/)

### MsgLoop

A **WhatsApp sender for Android** — one message, sent many times, right now or on a schedule.

- **Schedule for later** — pick a date and time; an exact alarm sends when you said it would.
- **A queue that survives anything** — tasks live on the phone, through restarts and app kills.
- **Private by design** — no account, no server, no tracking.

[Open MsgLoop's site](https://nadeem-dev-apps.pages.dev/products/msgloop/)

### CV Creator Pro

A **one-page CV maker in the browser** — fill it in, pick a look, download a clean A4 PDF. No account, no signup, no install.

- **In-browser, private** — your CV never leaves your device.
- **Live preview** — see the page exactly as the PDF comes out.
- **A4 PDF download** — clean, printable, ready to send.

Live app: https://cv-creator-pro.nadeem-devapps.workers.dev/

[Open CV Creator Pro's site](https://nadeem-dev-apps.pages.dev/products/cv-creator-pro/)

### Nydrex Suite

An **Electron desktop suite for retail** — point of sale and inventory, with customer and supplier ledgers and reporting. Windows desktop app.

- **Point of sale and inventory** — sales, stock, and suppliers in one place.
- **Ledgers** — customer and supplier accounts, tracked properly.
- **Reporting** — the numbers behind the shop, readable at a glance.

Windows desktop app — no live site.

### Brew & Bean Cafe

An **animated cafe site with a menu and cart** — orders are sent to the cafe as a WhatsApp message. An assignment project, fully working end to end.

- **Menu and cart** — browse, add, order.
- **WhatsApp order flow** — the order lands as a message, ready to confirm.
- **Animated and responsive** — built to feel like a real cafe site.

Live site: https://brew-and-bean.nadeem-devapps.workers.dev/

[Open Brew & Bean Cafe's site](https://nadeem-dev-apps.pages.dev/products/brew-and-bean/)
