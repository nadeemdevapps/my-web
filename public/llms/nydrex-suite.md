# Point of sale, inventory, and ledgers — on Windows.

Nydrex Suite is an **Electron desktop suite for retail** — point of sale and inventory, with customer and supplier ledgers and reporting.

Windows desktop app — v2.2.0 — no live site.

## What it does

- **Point of sale and inventory** — sales, stock, and suppliers in one place.
- **Ledgers** — customer and supplier accounts, tracked properly.
- **Reporting** — the numbers behind the shop, readable at a glance.
