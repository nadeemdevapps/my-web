# CV Creator Pro

> Markdown version of [the CV Creator Pro site](https://nadeem-dev-apps.pages.dev/products/cv-creator-pro/). CV Creator Pro is a one-page CV maker that runs entirely in the browser, by Nadeem Mazhar (Nadeem Dev Apps).

# A one-page CV maker in the browser.

Fill it in, pick a look, download a clean A4 PDF. No account, no signup, no install.

Live app: https://cv-creator-pro.nadeem-devapps.workers.dev/

## What it does

- **In-browser, private** — your CV never leaves your device.
- **Live preview** — see the page exactly as the PDF comes out.
- **A4 PDF download** — clean, printable, ready to send.

## Your details

Everything a CV needs, in one form: name, job title, photo, and contact info; About me, Passion, and Interests; Experience and Education with a short note each; Skills and Languages rated on a 1–5 scale. "Fill sample" loads example data; "Clear" resets the form. "Copy AI prompt" copies a ready-made interview prompt — paste it into any LLM, let it interview you step by step, then copy its answers back into the form. There is no AI backend; the interview happens outside the app.

## Six themes, five templates

Six colour sets: **Paper & Ink, Navy & Sun, Soft Sky, Forest, Terracotta, and Slate.** Five layouts: **Editorial, Sidebar, Elegant, Brutalist, and Classic.** Every template works with every theme.

## One-page A4 PDF

- **Single A4 page** — the finished CV downloads as one A4 page (210 × 297 mm), never two.
- **Limits keep it on the page** — word limits and entry limits, so the CV cannot grow past one page.
- **Measured before download** — the download step measures the rendered page and warns you if content would be clipped.
- **An honest caveat** — the PDF is a raster image: it looks exactly like the preview, but the text is not selectable and ATS parsers will not read it. If you need machine-readable text, use your browser's own Save as PDF from the preview, or paste the text into a plain-text CV.

## Saved in your browser

Every edit is saved in the browser's own storage on your device, and restored when you reload the page. Saved data is checked before it is used, and anything unknown falls back to a safe default. If saved data ever misbehaves, the error page offers a "Reset saved data" button.

## Private by design

No server. No account. No upload. Nothing leaves your machine — there is no backend, no analytics on your CV data, and no tracking. Your photo stays local. One third-party request: the page loads fonts from Google Fonts. Nothing else leaves your machine.
