# Tsumly

> Markdown version of [the Tsumly site](https://nadeem-dev-apps.pages.dev/products/tsumly/). Tsumly is a local-first personal expense and loan ledger for Android, by Nadeem Mazhar (Nadeem Dev Apps).

# Every amount adds up.

Tsumly is a **local-first** personal expense and loan ledger for Android: your money stays on your device — **no account, no cloud by default** — with an optional encrypted Google Drive backup. It tracks **income, expenses and transfers** between accounts, and its **smart multi-loan repayment split** spreads one repayment across several loans, while **pre-app loans** record older loans without moving your balance.

Free. Android. No account needed.

## Why Tsumly?

The name is inspired by the Japanese verb **tsumu** (積む) — to stack, to accumulate, to build up over time.

## What Tsumly does best

- **Smart repayment split** — one repayment, split across every loan it covers — never merged, smallest first, overpayment blocked.
- **Pre-app loans** — loans from before the app still count — recorded without moving your balance, and flippable later.
- **Person profiles** — people, not just numbers — per-person totals, a guarded archive, and a full ledger per person.
- **Backup & privacy** — your money stays yours — local-first, encrypted backup, no tracking, no account.

The full detail lives on the [Features page](https://nadeem-dev-apps.pages.dev/products/tsumly/features/).

## Built by Nadeem

Tsumly is built by **Nadeem Mazhar**, a solo developer, as **Nadeem Dev Apps**. It started as his own problem — a ledger that never added up — and it is built the way he builds everything: local-first, and honest about what it does.
